82,518 wallets.
Three days until 82,281 of their cats die.

Emogotchi is an on-chain tamagotchi on Monad — the art, the meters and the whole record live in the contract. It was airdropped to 82,423 Monad wallets at launch. On 24 September almost every untouched cat starves on-chain, permanently, and the survivors become the rarest thing in the collection. We want to put a MON airdrop behind that moment.

The Great Starvation · 24 Sep 2026, 21:16 UTC

A deadline the contract enforces, not us.

Every airdropped cat got a seven-day welcome, then a 48-hour hunger clock. 82,281 cats have never been fed once and die within a five-minute window on Wednesday. Nothing can stop it — the contract is immutable and ownerless. Death is written to the cat forever.

82,439 → ~158 Cats that have never died. Today that is 99.9% of the collection and means nothing. On Thursday it is about 0.19% — and it can never be minted, bought back or repaired.
The single decision that matters: snapshot after 24 September. A snapshot taken today rewards 82,439 wallets for doing nothing. Taken Thursday, it rewards ~158 wallets for a week of daily attention — and gives every sponsor a story with a clock on it.

Engagement — the honest picture

What is real

  • 242 wallets have paid to care for a pet — 164 on cats, 78 on froks.
  • 294 wallets active in the last 7 days; 111 in the last 24 hours.
  • 1,385 pets given — gas-only, so pure affection.
  • Retention is daily by design: meters drain over 24 hours, so caring is a habit, not a one-off.

What is not, and why that is the point

Of 82,518 cat holders, about 0.2% have ever touched their pet. Almost everyone was airdropped a cat and never came back.

That is precisely the asset. 82,281 wallets are one notification away from a dead pet and a reason to return — and a funded airdrop is the notification. Nobody has to acquire an audience here; it already exists, holds the NFT, and is about to have something happen to it.

Why the moment works

A deadline nobody controls

The starvation is contract-enforced and public. No one — including us — can delay, cancel or fake it. Campaigns built on real deadlines do not need urgency manufactured.

Scarcity that cannot be gamed

You cannot buy your way into "never died" after Wednesday. The cohort closes permanently and is verifiable by anyone from chain state.

No wallet needed to join

Since 20 Sep a visitor can make a real Monad account from Face ID — built on mera by Category Labs — and we cover the gas for their first actions. A sponsor's audience can go from a tweet to on-chain in four taps.

What we are asking for

  • MON to fund the airdrop pool, paid to holders by tier. Amount and thresholds set with sponsors.
  • Coordinated announcement. Every sponsor posts on the same day; the campaign runs to the snapshot, then pays out.
  • Co-branding across the event — the campaign page, the claim flow and the post-mortem numbers.
What sponsors get back: a verifiable, on-chain audience of 82,518 Monad wallets, a reason for them to transact on a specific day, and a story with a countdown that the chain itself enforces.

Verify any of this yourself

Everything above is public. Nothing here depends on trusting us.
